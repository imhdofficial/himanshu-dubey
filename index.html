05 / ABOUT

Design sense.
Developer brain.

I like products where visual judgement and implementation judgement have to coexist. That usually means fewer decorative decisions, more intentional states, and a lot of attention to what happens between the obvious screens.

I’m especially interested in interfaces that simplify technical, operational or commerce-heavy journeys without making them feel generic.
